Profile

My work sits at the junction of molecular biology and bioinformatics — using sequence- and structure-level computational tools to investigate questions in signal transduction, then returning to bench techniques to test them. This dual orientation pairs hands-on wet-lab grounding (DNA extraction, PCR, gel electrophoresis) with structural modelling in PyMOL and sequence analysis via BLAST, forming a feedback loop between computational hypothesis generation and experimental validation. I'm working toward research roles that build on this loop, particularly within cheminformatics and AI-driven drug discovery applied to disease-relevant signaling pathways.
